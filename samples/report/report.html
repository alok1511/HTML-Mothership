Methodology

How to reproduce and interpret the numbers.

Source and grain

All business figures are synthetic, generated for this layout. Use the Data explorer to reconcile totals to the contributing records.

Measures and denominators

Comparisons and scenarios

A rate difference is reported in percentage points. The scatter and matrix are descriptive. They can highlight an association or concentration without identifying its cause.

Map boundaries and joins

Shapes are matched to reporting states by name or ISO state code in the selected attribute. Unmatched states stay uncoloured. Rates are recomputed from the selected rows for each geography.

Bundled shapes cover the six sample states and were simplified from Natural Earth Admin 1. Use your approved boundary layer for publication.

FAQ

Use these answers when you hand over the report.

What changes when I filter the report?

Every chart, key inference and “So what” statement uses the filtered rows. Some reports use the latest selected snapshot or tenure for the headline. The active basis is shown above the KPIs.

What does the scenario assume?

How do I load a boundary file?

Open the Map tab and choose a WGS84 GeoJSON file or a ZIP with one polygon shapefile and its matching .dbf and .prj. Pick the attribute containing the state names. Check the match count before reading the map.

Can I audit the figures offline?

Yes. Open Data explorer, sort or page through the records, and download a CSV of the entire filtered slice. See Methodology before summing snapshots or cohort observations.
